## 🚨 Common Rejection Reasons & Solutions

### iOS App Store
1. **App Completeness**: Ensure no placeholder content
2. **Metadata Accuracy**: Description must match functionality  
3. **Design Guidelines**: Follow Human Interface Guidelines
4. **Privacy Policy**: Must be accessible and comprehensive
5. **App Performance**: No crashes or major bugs

### Google Play Store
1. **Metadata Policy**: Accurate descriptions and screenshots
2. **Content Policy**: No prohibited content
3. **Technical Requirements**: 64-bit support, target API
4. **Privacy Policy**: Required for apps collecting data
5. **Permissions**: Only request necessary permissions
